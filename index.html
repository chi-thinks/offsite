Offsite is a theory and poetry journal whose name is the english translation of the french horlieu, Alain Badiou’s term for that which eludes the ordinary order of things. We welcome work that exists on the fringes of thought, affect, and expression, and reject whatever conforms to their status quo. Have something (in)appropriate? Submit it to OffsiteJournal@proton.me.
